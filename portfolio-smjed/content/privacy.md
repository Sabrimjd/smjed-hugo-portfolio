This website (smjed.net) is a personal portfolio. It collects **no personal data** and sets **no tracking cookies**.

## What this site does not do

- No analytics or visitor tracking.
- No advertising cookies.
- No forms, no comments, no user accounts.
- No data selling or sharing with third parties.

## Hosting and technical logs

The site is served as static files. Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps) may be processed by the hosting provider for security and availability purposes only, and are retained for a short period. This is covered by the legitimate interest basis of the GDPR (Article 6(1)(f)).

## Third-party content

Embedded fonts and assets are self-hosted. The site embeds no third-party scripts that profile visitors.

## Your rights

No personal data is stored by this site, so there is nothing to access, rectify or delete. For any question: [contact@smjed.net](mailto:contact@smjed.net).
